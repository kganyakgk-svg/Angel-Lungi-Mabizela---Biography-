## Profile / Testimonial
As a creative, I believe that creativity is the quality that I bring to a particular activity. Anything can be creative—people can dance, sing, or paint in an uncreative way, unless it is a quality that you bring to the activity of what you are doing. I look at things with an inner approach and perceive them in a creative way. In my view, a person walking with intent, cleaning a space mindfully, or even smiling is still a form of creativity. A man sitting silently in non-doing can be a creative act; a Buddha sitting is the most divine creative act. While people dance, sing, paint, and practice various kinds of art, I am one of those who focus on creating my own masterpiece in writing and content creation. I also strongly believe that teamwork is one of the most vital creative muscles that any production cannot do without.
